How to find your exact Dyson model

Before ordering, confirm the series (not just the colour or "Animal" name). The fastest way:

  • Look on the main body / bin behind the trigger for a small label with the model and a serial number.
  • On the original battery, read the printed part number (e.g. 965874-02, 967834-05, 969352-02).
  • Dyson also stamps an SV code on the main housing (SV03 = V6, SV10 = V8, SV11 = V7, SV12 = V10).
Rule of thumb: If the battery is held by 2–3 small Phillips screws, it is a screw-in pack. If it releases with a red/blue button, it is a click-in pack (V11/V15). The two types are not interchangeable.

Dyson DC35, DC31, DC34 — Type A vs Type B

The DC35 generation is the most commonly mis-ordered battery in Australia. There are two physically different batteries, and they do not fit each other.

Type A — button-release

  • Fits: DC31, DC34, and early/button DC35 (and DC35 with a clip-release battery)
  • Voltage: 22.2V Li-ion
  • Original capacity: ~1,500–2,000 mAh
  • Released by pressing a button on the handle
  • OEM part refs: 967863-03/04, 917083-01

Type B — screw-mounted

  • Fits: later DC35, DC44, DC45 (screw-in battery)
  • Voltage: 22.2V Li-ion
  • Original capacity: ~2,000 mAh
  • Held in by 2 screws
  • OEM part refs: 917083-01 (B variants), 17083-2811, 18172-01-04
Ordering tip: If you can't tell which you have, photograph the battery with screws/button visible and send it to the retailer before buying. Ordering the wrong Type is the #1 reason for returns. AussieBatt's DC35 Type A listing shows both variants.

Dyson V11 (SV14–SV18)

The V11 is the trickiest: early Torque Drive units use a screw-in battery (970145-02), while later Absolute / Outsize units use a click-in battery (970938-01). Identify yours before ordering.

Compatibility FAQ

Can a V8 battery fit a V6 or V7?
No. Although all three are 21.6V and screw-in, the battery housing shape differs. Always buy the pack listed for your exact series.
Will a V10 battery work in a V11?
No. V10 is 25.2V and screw-in; V11 is also 25.2V but uses a different mount (screw-in or click-in). They are not interchangeable.
Is the V11 click-in battery the same as V15?
Yes — the later V11 Absolute/Outsize and the V15 Detect share the same click-in battery (part 970938-01 / 970425-01).
What if my model isn't listed?
Send your model + serial (or a photo of the battery label) to AussieBatt support and they'll confirm the right pack.
