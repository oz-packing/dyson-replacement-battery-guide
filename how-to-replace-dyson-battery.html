How to Replace Your Dyson Battery (Step-by-Step)

Most Dyson handstick batteries take under 5 minutes to swap with a single Phillips screwdriver — no technician, no special tools. Below are clear, model-by-model tutorials for the DC35 (Type A), V6, V7, V8, V10, V11 and V15, plus what to do if something doesn't line up.

Before you start — tools & safety

✓ Phillips (#1/#2) screwdriver ✓ Small tray for screws ✓ Your new compatible battery ✓ 5 minutes, a clean surface
Safety first: Always remove the vacuum from the charger and detach any wand/tool before working on the battery. Work in a dry area. If the old battery is swollen, hot or leaking, stop and dispose of it at a BatteryWorld or council e-waste point — do not puncture it.

Not sure which battery you need? Open the compatibility chart first.

Dyson DC35 (Type A) — button-release swap

Type A batteries release with a clip/button, so there are no screws on the battery itself — but you may need to free the housing.

  1. Unplug the charger and remove the wand and any tool head.
  2. Locate the red/blue release button near the top of the battery, behind the trigger.
  3. Press and hold the button while sliding the battery downwards until it releases.
  4. Align the new Type A battery with the rail and slide it up until it clicks and locks.
  5. Charge fully (around 3.5 hours) before first use, then run a full clean to calibrate.
If your DC35 battery is screw-mounted instead, you have a Type B — follow the screw-out steps in the V6 section (same 2-screw principle). DC35 Type A battery →

Dyson V6 — 2-screw battery

  1. Remove from charger; detach wand and tools.
  2. Turn the machine so the battery faces up. You'll see two Phillips screws on the battery's lower edge.
  3. Loosen and remove both screws (keep them safe).
  4. Slide the old battery down and out of the housing.
  5. Slide the new V6 battery in, align the screw holes, and refit the two screws finger-tight then snug — don't over-tighten.
  6. Charge fully before use. V6 battery →

Dyson V7 — 2-screw battery

Identical process to the V6 (the V7 battery sits in the same style cradle).

  1. Remove from charger and strip off attachments.
  2. Find the two Phillips screws on the battery base.
  3. Remove screws, slide old battery out.
  4. Fit the new V7 battery, replace and lightly tighten screws.
  5. Full charge, then a calibration run. V7 battery →

Dyson V8 — 2-screw battery

  1. Power off, remove from dock, detach wand/tool.
  2. The V8 battery has two screws at its base — loosen both.
  3. Slide the pack downward and out.
  4. Insert the new V8 battery, align screw holes, refit and tighten.
  5. Charge 3.5–5 hours; run a full clean to condition cells. V8 battery →

Dyson V10 — 3-screw battery

The V10 battery is larger and uses three screws.

  1. Remove from charger and detach all accessories.
  2. Turn the vacuum over to expose the battery; remove the three Phillips screws.
  3. Carefully slide the battery out — it's a tighter fit than V6–V8, so wiggle gently.
  4. Seat the new V10 battery, line up all three holes, and replace screws.
  5. Charge fully; the V10 takes a little longer to reach 100%. V10 battery →

Dyson V11 — screw-in OR click-in

Check which you have (see the chart):

Screw-in V11 (early Torque Drive)

  1. Remove from charger, strip accessories.
  2. Unscrew the three screws holding the battery.
  3. Slide out the old pack, fit the new screw-in V11 battery, refit screws.

Click-in V11 (later Absolute/Outsize)

  1. Press the red release button at the battery base.
  2. Slide the old battery out.
  3. Slide the new click-in pack in until it clicks. No tools required.

Dyson V15 Detect — click-in

  1. Power off and remove from charger.
  2. Press the red release button on the battery and slide it out.
  3. Slide the new V15 click-in battery in until it locks (shares fit with V11 click-in).
  4. Charge fully. The V15 LCD will show the new battery status.

Installation FAQ

Do I need special tools?
No. A standard Phillips screwdriver handles every screw-in model. Click-in models (V11/V15) need no tools at all.
Will fitting a new battery void my Dyson warranty?
If your machine is outside Dyson's warranty period, replacing the battery has no warranty implications. If it's still under warranty, contact Dyson first — but most batteries fail well after the 2-year cover ends.
How long does a replacement take to charge?
Typically 3.5 hours for V6–V11 and a little longer for high-capacity V10/V15 packs. Always charge to 100% before first use.
My new battery dies fast — is it faulty?
Usually not. Lithium cells need 2–3 full cycles to "learn" capacity. If it's still poor after that, contact the retailer for a replacement under warranty.
