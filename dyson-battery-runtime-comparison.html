Capacity (mAh) is only half the story

A bigger mAh number means more stored energy, but runtime also depends on motor draw (voltage) and how hard the vacuum is working. The V10/V11/V15 run longer mostly because their 25.2V platform and efficient motors deliver more cleaning per watt.

OEM vs high-capacity aftermarket — which to choose?

Choose OEM-style (standard mAh) if:

  • You want the exact original runtime and weight.
  • Your current battery is fine but end-of-life and you just want a like-for-like swap.
  • Budget is the priority for a light-use machine.

Choose high-capacity aftermarket if:

  • You have a larger Australian home or pets and need more minutes per charge.
  • You want to delay the next replacement (more cells = slower wear per cycle).
  • You want the best value per run-time-hour.
Buy from a reputable Australian supplier with certified cells and a real BMS. Cheap no-name packs often overstate mAh and lack safety circuitry — see our avoid-fakes guide.

Why runtime drops over time

Lithium-ion cells degrade with every charge cycle and with heat. In a hot Australian summer, leaving a Dyson on a sunny windowsill or in a hot car accelerates this. Typical lifespan:

  • Year 1–2: Minimal loss — near full runtime.
  • Year 2–3: Noticeable drop, especially in MAX mode.
  • Year 3–4: Often half the original runtime; replacement recommended.

Good news: a fresh battery restores like-new runtime. See our care guide to make the next one last longer.

Runtime FAQ

Will a higher mAh battery damage my Dyson?
No. The motor draws only what it needs; a larger pack just supplies more total energy. Quality packs include a BMS to protect both battery and machine.
Why is my V10 battery not holding charge like when new?
Normal lithium-ion wear, made worse by heat and deep discharges. After ~3 years, replacement is the practical fix.
Does Eco mode really double runtime?
In most Dyson models, Eco/low mode roughly doubles runtime versus middle mode and is ideal for hard floors and light debris.
Can I rebuild my old battery instead of replacing it?
Not recommended for safety — Dyson packs are sealed with integrated BMS and puncture risk. A new certified pack is safer and cheaper than a DIY rebuild.
